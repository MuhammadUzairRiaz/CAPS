21-step compression and decompression

Alternating NVT heating, NVT cooling and NPT compression up to a maximum pressure, then decompression stages, then a final NPT stage at the target conditions. It relaxes the density of glassy polymer cells far faster than plain NPT at room temperature.

$$\begin{gathered}\text{21 stages: heat to } T_\mathrm{max}, \text{ cool to } T_\mathrm{final}, \text{ compress at } 0.02, 0.6, 1.0\,P_\mathrm{max} \dots \\ \text{decompress at } 0.5, 0.1, 0.01\,P_\mathrm{max}, \text{ final NPT at } P_\mathrm{final}\end{gathered}$$

Larsen et al. 2011, Table 1

When to use it

Glassy polymer cells before property calculations. Rubbers far above Tg equilibrate with plain NPT. For long runs on a cluster, Export writes any CAPS protocol for LAMMPS (protocol Equilibration protocol): each stage its own fix and run in the same ensembles, temperatures and pressures (Nosé–Hoover), every length scaled by -var scale for a quick check, then NPT production with density.dat; the push-off stages limit each step to 0.1 Å instead of capping the Lennard-Jones force.

Bond exchange moves during MD

Bonds that break and form while CAPS's own dynamics runs, for every exchange chemistry. Every few steps, Monte Carlo moves change the topology, and the force field follows from its own terms by type. Swap: two bonds trade partners, as LAMMPS fix bond/swap does (associative exchange; transesterification, disulfide metathesis, bead-spring vitrimers). Shift: a pivot hands a bond from one residue to another within reach, with a valence per residue (multivalent linkers, polysulfide sulfur). Bind: two sites bond or a bond breaks, weighed by a binding constant (dissociative exchange; Diels–Alder, stickers). Each move is Metropolis–Hastings with its proposal counts, so the topologies are sampled with their Boltzmann weights. A barrier slows every move alike without biasing them. The energy change is computed exactly from the atoms within three bonds of the change, which is all a change can affect. The energy the moves add is kept out of the conserved quantity.

$$p_\mathrm{acc} = e^{-E_a/k_\mathrm{B}T}\,\min\!\left[1,\ \frac{q_\mathrm{rev}}{q_\mathrm{fwd}}\,K^{\pm 1}\,e^{-\Delta U/k_\mathrm{B}T}\right],\qquad \Delta U = U_\mathrm{cluster}^\mathrm{after} - U_\mathrm{cluster}^\mathrm{before}$$

q: the probability of proposing the move and its reverse (swap: the four ways of picking the same swap; shift: attackers before and after; bind: either site picked first); K = exp(ε/k_B T) for bind, + on binding and − on breaking

Departure from the reference
LAMMPS bond/swap accepts on the Metropolis factor alone and tries one swap per processor and call; CAPS adds the proposal ratio (exact detailed balance) and tries as many moves as asked. Swap labels: like LAMMPS, a1/b1 and a2/b2 must share them.

Healing-test builders and exchange protocols

The structures a healing test starts from and the runs that measure bond exchange, all with the moves of the exchange page. Builders: a cut removes every bond crossing a plane (by the minimum image); a gap cuts and then moves the upper side away, lengthening the cell, for a weld; a crack removes the atoms within a sphere with their bonds; fragments are cuts at the mid-planes of chosen axes, the pieces a recycling run joins again. Protocols: a step strain stretches the cell along one axis at constant volume and follows the normal stress difference as exchange relaxes it, with a KWW fit; a Tv scan runs NPT at each temperature with exchange and fits a hinge to the specific volume, and an Arrhenius law to the time between accepted moves; a heal run counts the bonds bridging the two sides of a cut, and those crossing its plane, after each chunk of MD (at constant pressure to close a gap). Creep takes the same moves through the creep run's topology hook. Each run hands the force field the moves left to the next, so protocols chain.

$$\sigma(t) = \sigma_\parallel - \tfrac12\left(\sigma_{\perp 1} + \sigma_{\perp 2}\right) \approx \sigma_0\, e^{-(t/\tau)^{\beta}},\qquad \langle\tau\rangle = \frac{\tau}{\beta}\,\Gamma\!\left(\frac{1}{\beta}\right),\qquad v(T) = v_0 + b_\mathrm{low}\min(T - T_v, 0) + b_\mathrm{high}\max(T - T_v, 0)$$

stress from the pressure tensor (atm × 0.101325 = MPa); the strain keeps the volume: the axis × (1 + ε), the other two × 1/√(1 + ε); the hinge is Tg in a network without exchange
