Damped shifted force (DSF)

A pairwise alternative to Ewald sums: the Coulomb interaction is damped by erfc and shifted so that both the energy and the force go to zero at the cut-off. It converges to Ewald results for condensed phases at modest cost.

$$E = q_i q_j\left[\frac{\operatorname{erfc}(\alpha r)}{r} - \frac{\operatorname{erfc}(\alpha r_c)}{r_c} + \left(\frac{\operatorname{erfc}(\alpha r_c)}{r_c^2} + \frac{2\alpha}{\sqrt{\pi}}\,\frac{e^{-\alpha^2 r_c^2}}{r_c}\right)(r - r_c)\right], \qquad r < r_c$$

Fennell & Gezelter 2006

SymbolMeaningIn CAPS
αdamping parameter0.2 Å⁻¹
r_ccut-off, shared with van der Waals10 Å (set per run)
erfccomplementary error functioninside the cut-off erfc(αr)/r = 1/r − erf(αr)/r, the second from a cubic Hermite table in r² (error below 10⁻¹² of it); Abramowitz–Stegun 7.1.26 (|error| < 1.5 × 10⁻⁷, as LAMMPS) only for the shift constants at r_c and for frozen and excluded pairs beyond the cut-off

When to use it

The default electrostatics in CAPS: cheap, no grid, fine for neutral bulk polymers. Use smooth PME for ionic systems, interfaces with net dipoles, or when comparing with Ewald-based codes.

Smooth particle-mesh Ewald (SPME)

The Ewald sum split into a short-ranged real-space part and a smooth reciprocal part evaluated on a grid: charges are spread with B-splines, transformed by FFT, multiplied by the influence function and gathered back as forces. The threaded version keeps the transform of the real charge grid for half the frequencies; at an even grid's Nyquist index the influence function is the mean over both signs, so it is even in m in any cell.

$$E_\mathrm{rec} = \frac{1}{2\pi V}\sum_{m \ne 0}\frac{\exp(-\pi^2 m^2/\beta^2)}{m^2}\,B(m)\,\left|F(Q)(m)\right|^2, \qquad \operatorname{erfc}(\beta r_c) = \epsilon_\mathrm{rel}$$

Essmann et al. 1995

Pair forces in mixed precision (cluster kernel)

Atoms are sorted into columns and cut into clusters of four. The pair list holds pairs of clusters, each with its periodic image and a 16-bit mask of the atom pairs that interact, and is pruned every few steps to r_c + 0.8 Å. The kernel takes the four atoms of a j cluster in one SIMD register against each i atom, in single precision with positions relative to each cluster's origin; energies, virial and forces leave it in double. The Ewald (or DSF) real-space term comes from rational fits of erf(√u)/√u and its derivative, so no exponential is evaluated per pair.

$$\frac{\operatorname{erfc}(\kappa r)}{r} = \frac{1}{r} - \kappa\, g_V(\kappa^2 r^2), \qquad -\frac{1}{r}\frac{d}{dr}\frac{\operatorname{erfc}(\kappa r)}{r} = \frac{1}{r^3} + \kappa^3 g_F(\kappa^2 r^2), \qquad g \approx \frac{P_6(t)}{Q_4(t)},\; t = \frac{\kappa^2 r^2}{16}$$

the cluster scheme of Páll & Hess 2013; fits made for CAPS

When to use it

Molecular dynamics and equilibration with plain 12-6 or class II 9-6 Lennard-Jones and SPME, DSF or no charges (OPLS-AA, PCFF, COMPASS, CVFF, GAFF): on by default for MD (caps md --precision double turns it off). Other pair forms, single-point energies and minimisation keep the double-precision loop. NEON on Arm, SSE2 or AVX2 with FMA on x86-64, chosen at run time.

Ewald summation (reference)

The plain Ewald sum over reciprocal-lattice vectors. CAPS keeps it as the reference the PME kernel is tested against; runs use PME.

$$E = E_\mathrm{real} + E_\mathrm{rec} + E_\mathrm{self}, \qquad E_\mathrm{self} = -\frac{\beta}{\sqrt{\pi}}\sum_i q_i^2$$

Ewald 1921

PME for slab cells: the vacuum correction

A film or surface with vacuum above it, in a periodic cell with c along z, still feels its own images along z through a plain Ewald or PME sum, and the energy then depends on how much vacuum the cell has. With the slab correction on, the reciprocal sum runs on the cell lengthened along z by a factor (3 is usual) and the dipole term of Yeh and Berkowitz is added with its forces. For a slab with a net charge, the terms of Ballenegger, Arnold and Cerdà keep the energy unchanged when the slab moves along z. V′ and L′_z are the volume and height of the lengthened box, the box the Ewald sum runs on, as in LAMMPS's kspace_modify slab. The LAMMPS input gets boundary p p f and kspace_modify slab, the GROMACS parameters ewald-geometry = 3dc (GROMACS lengthens nothing itself, so its cell should hold the vacuum). The correction gives no virial: the pressure along z means nothing for a slab, and x and y keep theirs.

$$E_{\mathrm{corr}} = \frac{2\pi k}{V'}\left(M_z^2 - Q\sum_i q_i z_i^2 - \frac{Q^2 L_z'^2}{12}\right),\qquad M_z = \sum_i q_i z_i,\qquad F_{iz} = -\frac{4\pi k}{V'}\,q_i\left(M_z - Q z_i\right)$$

k Coulomb's constant (332.06 kcal Å/(mol e²)); Q the net charge; z inside the cell; V′ = factor × V, L′_z = factor × L_z

Departure from the reference
c must lie along z and a, b in the xy plane. No virial from the correction. Atoms must stay inside the cell along z (the vacuum keeps them there); one that crosses is wrapped, which a slab should never need.
