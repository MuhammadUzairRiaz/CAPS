Adjusting computed charges

After a charge method: atoms equivalent in the bond graph share their mean charge, every charge can be scaled, and the total brought to a chosen value — evenly, or in proportion to each atom's |q| so small charges barely move.

$$q_i \leftarrow q_i + \frac{Q - \sum_j q_j}{N}\quad\text{or}\quad q_i + (Q - \textstyle\sum_j q_j)\,\frac{|q_i|}{\sum_j |q_j|}$$

classes from the element and the neighbours' classes, refined until stable (Morgan's extended connectivity); Q defaults to the formal charge × s

When to use it

Symmetric groups that a method left unequal, charge-scaled ions (the electronic continuum correction, s ≈ 0.75–0.8), a fragment's small residual charge before building a polymer from it.
