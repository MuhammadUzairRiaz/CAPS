Get started

The window every module lives in: open files, find pages with ⌘K, and follow the pipeline strip.

StartType a SMILES or a file path, pick a builder, reopen a project, or follow a guided example.5 stepsStudioThe 3D view, project tree, inspector and quick analysis; the pipeline strip shows how far a structure has got.6 steps

Build

Make the structure: molecules, polymers, amorphous cells, packed boxes, crystals, slabs, fillers and solvated systems.

Molecule builderDraw or type a SMILES and get 3D conformers: monomers, curatives, solvents and additives.4 stepsPolymer builder114 repeat units and 21 copolymers, or your own SMILES; architecture, sequence, composition, tacticity and end groups.6 stepsPolymer cellGrow amorphous chains into a periodic cell at a target density, with the force field assigned when growth ends.6 stepsBlendSeveral polymers grown together in one cell: mixed, as two slabs, or as a droplet.4 stepsPackingFill a box — or the space around a structure — with molecules, with no two closer than a tolerance.5 stepsSolvationSolvent and ions around a solute: box shape, solvent model, salt concentration or neutralisation.4 stepsCrystalSpace group, lattice and sites; CIF import; supercells, primitive and Niggli cells, defects and nanowires.5 stepsSurface & interfaceCleave a crystal along (hkl), choose the termination, add vacuum, and grow a polymer film or brush on it.5 stepsNanostructureGraphene and h-BN sheets, nanotubes, particles and pores; graft functional groups; grow a polymer matrix around them.5 steps

BiomoleculePeptides from a sequence with their secondary structure, termini and protonation; DNA and RNA strands.5 steps

Prepare

Give the structure a force field, take out close contacts and bring it to the density and temperature you want.

Force fieldType every atom from SMARTS rules, set charges, fill any missing term and give each part of a composite its own force field.6 stepsMinimiseRemove close contacts, compress a low-density cell to its target and relax the box.5 stepsEquilibrateRun a published protocol such as Larsen's 21 steps, then production blocks until density, energy and Rg stop drifting.5 steps

Run

Analyse & export

Measure properties, write decks for LAMMPS, GROMACS and others, run jobs and draft the methods section.
