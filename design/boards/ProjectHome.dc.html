Methods section · generated from provenance

Amorphous cells of polystyrene (20 chains, degree of polymerisation 40, 12 840 atoms per cell) were built with CAPS 0.1.0. Atom types and charges were assigned from GAFF2 [1]. Chains were grown by configurational-bias Monte Carlo with 16 trial directions per step, using the full force-field energy [2, 3], at an initial density of 0.50 g/cm³, and minimised with L-BFGS [4] after a soft-core push-off [5]. Cells were equilibrated with the 21-step compression–decompression protocol (Pmax = 50 000 bar) [6], followed by 10 ns of NPT dynamics at 298 K and 1 atm using stochastic velocity rescaling [7] and stochastic cell rescaling [8]. Electrostatics used smooth PME with a relative tolerance of 10⁻⁵ [9]; Lennard-Jones interactions were truncated at 12 Å with analytic tail corrections.
