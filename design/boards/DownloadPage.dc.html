Chain Assembly and Packing Suite · 0.1.0

Build, pack and equilibrate polymer systems, with every step cited.

One self-contained program from monomer SMILES to an equilibrated cell. Its own engines for growth, packing, minimisation and dynamics, and input decks for LAMMPS and GROMACS that reproduce its energies.

What it does

Force-field-aware growthConfigurational-bias growth with the real force field, plus Theodorou–Suter with RIS statistics.
Packing without packmolRigid-body overlap minimisation with region constraints and a guaranteed minimum distance.
Its own MD engineVerlet and r-RESPA, CSVR and Nosé–Hoover, cell rescaling, SPME, LINCS.
Typing you can explainEvery atom type comes with the rule that assigned it. Missing parameters stop the run.
Provenance by defaultEvery file carries its inputs, versions, seeds, approximations and citations.
Benchmarks you can rerunOne command regenerates the validation tables, including where CAPS is slower.

How to cite

If CAPS contributes to your work, cite the software paper and the methods it used. CAPS writes the method references for you into every provenance manifest.

[Authors]. CAPS: Chain Assembly and Packing Suite. [Journal] ([year]). doi:[pending]

Coming from scripts?

CAPS reads YAML and JSON recipes. Steps that used packmol, LAMMPS or DL_FIELD now run on CAPS engines.
